# domains/minds/

**Charter:** Appendix A, Ruling 14 (Amendment A-9) — decision systems. *Decisions operate on
information, not reality* (Vol. II Ch. 4, invariant 6).

**Owns:** what a mind is committed to — its goal, the next step of its plan, its routines — and
the trace of why it chose (`mind.*`).

**Reads only:** the information layer's beliefs (`info.*`) and its own facts. Never reality:
a mind knows where it stands, how cold it is, and whether its way was blocked only as beliefs.

**Acts only through:** the intents anyone may propose — where to travel and how fast, what to
open (Ruling 13).

**Key contracts:** deterministic and utility-based (Vol. V Ch. 9 §9.3); no candidate is ever
drawn from reality, so a mind never walks to a place it does not know; every choice leaves a
trace.
