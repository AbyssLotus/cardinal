# domains/information/

**Charter:** Vol. II Ch. 4 — Information, as built by Amendment A-8. *Reality is universal.
Information is local.*

**Owns:** what each mind observes, believes, and remembers: what it is watching now
(`info.in_sight`), its beliefs about other things (`info.belief.*`, facts about a pair,
Amendment A-7), and what it feels of itself (`info.self.*`).

**Never owns:** the facts beliefs are about (Physical Reality, Living Systems, and the rest),
what is in view (Physical Reality publishes it), or what a mind decides (decision systems,
`domains/minds`).

**Key contracts:** observation reads reality and never changes it; beliefs are per mind,
carry their provenance (when, how), and go stale when not refreshed; nothing here imports a
domain.
